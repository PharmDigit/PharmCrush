Top 300 Drugs List for Pharmacy Students

This top 300 drugs list covers 400 high-yield medications for pharmacy students, PharmD students and NAPLEX candidates. It includes the commonly tested top 300 drugs plus key hospital, oncology and vaccine agents, grouped by therapeutic topic, with each drug’s generic name, a common brand name and its drug class.

Learning names and classes is only the first step. For the NAPLEX you also need each drug’s indications, contraindications and FDA boxed warnings, monitoring parameters, classic side effects and key drug interactions. PharmCrush turns all of that into a quick medication quiz game.
