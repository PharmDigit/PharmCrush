How to Play PharmCrush, the Top 300 Drugs Quiz Game

PharmCrush is a pharmacy learning game for the top 300 medications. A drug appears on the card and you answer its NAPLEX-style questions by tapping the correct candy tile. Answer them all to master the drug.

Try your first game in about 3 minutes
Free for your first 30 drugs · works in any browser.

Play PharmCrush

Your first game

  1. On the home screen, choose Classic.
  2. Under Questions per drug, choose 4 quick.
  3. Press Play and answer each question before the timer bar runs out.
  4. When the game ends, read the Review these list to learn what you missed.

Playing a drug

  • Read the question under the drug card, for example Q2/4 · Mechanism of action.
  • Correct: the tile bursts, you earn points and the next question drops in.
  • Wrong: the screen shakes, you lose a life and a short note explains what that answer really belongs to. Keep trying until you find the right one.
  • Time runs out: you lose a life and the correct answer glows.

Question types: what the drug quiz covers

Each drug can be asked up to ten kinds of questions, in this order:

  1. Brand name
  2. Drug class
  3. Mechanism of action
  4. Indication: what the drug is used for
  5. Black box warning or contraindication, including FDA boxed warnings
  6. Monitoring parameters: labs, drug levels and vital signs
  7. Key side effect: the classic medication side effects
  8. Drug interaction
  9. Patient counseling point
  10. Antidote or reversal agent, for drugs that have one

Choose 4 quick, 6 standard or All questions per drug. Question types you missed before come back first.

Game modes

  • Classic: endless levels. Every 4 drugs you level up, the timer gets faster and you get a life back.
  • Daily Challenge: the same 5 drugs for everyone today. Finish all 5 to grow your day streak.
  • Topic: only drugs from one area, such as cardiovascular, infectious diseases or psychiatry. See the top 300 drugs list for every topic.
  • Time Attack: 60 seconds on one clock with no lives. A wrong answer costs 5 seconds.

Scoring, combos and power-ups

  • 100 points for a first-try answer (50 after a mistake), plus a speed bonus of up to 100.
  • Combo multiplier: every 4 first-try answers in a row raise it, up to x5. A mistake resets it.
  • Drug Mastered bonus, plus +500 for a perfect drug.
  • Power-ups earned by answering in a row: 50/50 removes two wrong tiles, Freeze stops the timer for 8 seconds, and +1 Life gives back a heart.
  • Reverse bonus questions in Classic and Topic: the card shows a clue and you pick the drug. Worth +200, and a wrong answer costs no life.

After the game

The game-over screen shows your score, drugs mastered and accuracy, plus a Review these list with the correct answer for every question you missed. Missed drugs come back more often until you answer them right on the first try.

Controls

  • Tap a tile, or press 1–4 or A–D on a keyboard.
  • Power-ups: Q 50/50, W Freeze, E +1 Life. Esc pauses.
  • The speaker button turns sound on or off. Themes change colors only, never the questions or scoring.
